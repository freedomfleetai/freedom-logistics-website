The Platform

Five modules. One accountable system.

Every module works against the same managed asset record — so visibility, compliance, and execution stay in sync.

Asset360
Complete Asset Visibility

Full asset view for every individual asset, including documentation, federal inspection, registration, serial data, last known tire tread depth, repair requests, and historical/present work orders.

FreeSight
Analytics, Forecasting & Intelligence

Fleet health scoring, cost forecasting, heat mapping, asset density, utilization, empty/loaded status, and executive insights.

Free60
Compliance & Maintenance Execution

DOT, PM, BIT scheduling, compliance lifecycle management, repair planning, and work order execution.

FreeTrack
Telematics & Asset Monitoring

GPS, cargo, TPMS, door, sensor health, geofencing, dwell alerts, dormant-device monitoring, and device replacement workflows.

FreeAudit
Automated Quality Assurance

Agent-powered work order review for compliance, documentation, SRTs, approvals, invoice accuracy, and audit readiness.
